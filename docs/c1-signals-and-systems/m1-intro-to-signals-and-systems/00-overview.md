# Module 1.1 - Intro to Signals and System: Overview

<div id="ve-module-progress"></div>

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of the module, you should be able to:
- Describe what a signal is
- Explain the differences between discrete and continuous signals
- Describe the effects of quantisation
- Determine the periodicity of a signal
- Understand and apply properties of symmetry
- Calculate the power and energy of signals
